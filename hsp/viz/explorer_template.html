About these values

Si monomers are drawn as amber diamonds. Their values may not be reliable: the Si groups were fitted on 67 small Si compounds, only 7 of them measured, while these monomers are mostly TBS and TMS silyl ethers about four times larger.

Ra = √(4ΔδD² + ΔδP² + ΔδH²), in MPa½. Axes are plain δD, δP and δH, so on-screen distance is not Ra.
